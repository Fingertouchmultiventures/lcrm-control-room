Reads the comments on your live video so you can put a comment on screen with a click. Reply to people in Facebook itself (button below). You need a Facebook Page access token (see Help).

Open Facebook chat & reply
Help: how to get the token
  1. Open developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer while signed in as the page admin.
  2. Choose your app, then Get Page Access Token and pick the LCRM Church page. Allow pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_read_user_content.
  3. Copy the token here. Short tokens last 1-2 hours; a "long-lived" page token lasts much longer.
